Our Story

A Grill House Built
for Families

Dangormai started with one simple belief — great food doesn't need to be expensive. Nestled in the heart of Duliajan, we've been serving freshly grilled BBQ, crispy fries, and handcrafted snacks to families, friends, and food lovers who know good taste.

Our kitchen runs on real ingredients, real care, and a genuine love for the grill. Every dish is made fresh to order — because you deserve better than reheated.
